Privacy Policy for QueueFlow

We collect only the details required to book clinic visits, track live queue tokens, notify you of turn updates, and deliver payment receipts safely.

App: QueueFlow (com.queueflow.patient) · Last Updated: November 26, 2026

01 Information We Collect

Essential profile, booking, location, and live queue records.

  • Account & Identity Info: Full name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and generated Health ID when you register or sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Optional Health Preferences: Details you choose to store in your profile such as blood group, age, allergies, and emergency contact.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise device location (when granted) to measure clinic distance and display nearby clinic queue availability.
  • Bookings, Uploads & Device Tokens: Appointment slots, live queue tokens, clinic/doctor reviews, user-selected medical documents/images, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens.
  • Payment Records: Payment reference IDs, invoice amounts, and refund status metadata generated through Razorpay checkout (we never store raw card numbers or CVV).

02 How We Use Your Data

Powering clinic check-ins, real-time queues, and verified receipts.

  • Authenticate your account, reserve doctor consultation slots, and synchronize live queue token positions.
  • Send timely system and in-app notifications when your queue turn approaches or when a clinic updates your visit status.
  • Provide verifiable digital payment receipts, cancellation refund tracking, and authentic patient feedback.
  • Maintain app security, prevent duplicate/abusive bookings, and diagnose stability issues via Firebase Analytics.

03 Sharing With Clinics & Providers

Shared strictly to fulfill your chosen visit and infrastructure services.

  • Partner Clinics You Select: Partner clinics and assigned doctors receive your name, phone/email, selected service, check-in token, and booking details needed to manage your appointment and consultation queue.
  • Trusted Service Processors: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics) for secure hosting and Razorpay for encrypted payment processing.
  • No Data Selling: QueueFlow never sells, rents, or trades personal or health data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.

04 Security, Retention & Your Rights

Update, manage, or delete your account whenever you wish.

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and protected by Firebase authentication and security rules.
  • You can update profile details, health preferences, notifications, or submitted clinic reviews inside the app at any time.
  • You may request permanent deletion of your account and personal records at any time—see our Account & Data Deletion Guide.
  • Children's Privacy: QueueFlow is not directed to children under 13 without parental/guardian authorization.
