Account & Data Deletion

How to request permanent deletion of your QueueFlow patient account and associated personal data.

App: QueueFlow (com.queueflow.patient) · Developer: Muhammed Saad

01 How to Request Deletion

Follow either option below to delete your account.

  • In-App: Open QueueFlow → go to the Profile tab → select Account Controls / Delete Account if available on your build.
  • By Email Request: Send an email from your registered account address to muhammedsaad007700@gmail.com with the subject QueueFlow Account Deletion Request and include your registered email or phone number.
  • Requests are verified and completed within 7 business days.

02 Data Deleted vs. Retained

What happens once your deletion request is processed.

  • Permanently Deleted: Firebase Auth credentials, user profile (name, email, phone, Health ID), optional health info (allergies, blood group, emergency contact), favorite clinics, notifications, and FCM push tokens.
  • Temporarily Retained (Legal/Billing): Minimal transaction/invoice records associated with paid consultations or Razorpay refunds may be retained only as required for financial accounting, audit, or fraud prevention compliance, and anonymized from your active profile.
