QueueFlow Patient Terms

Terms & Conditions

Transparent guidelines covering appointment scheduling, live queue tokens, consultation fees, and patient conduct on QueueFlow.

App: QueueFlow (com.queueflow.patient) · Effective: November 26, 2026

01 Platform Use & Eligibility

Accurate patient identity keeps clinic queues reliable.

  • Provide accurate contact details when creating your account or booking a medical consultation.
  • Do not submit automated, duplicate, or fraudulent bookings that block care slots for other patients.
  • Keep your login credentials secure; you are responsible for actions taken under your signed-in account.

02 Bookings & Live Queue Rules

Queue tracking unlocks on your visit day during clinic operating hours.

  • Booked consultations allow live queue tracking on the consultation date once the clinic opens operating hours.
  • Estimated wait times adjust dynamically as doctors complete consultations or clinics pause queues.
  • Arrive or check in promptly when your token is called to avoid automatic skip or reschedule.

03 Fees, Receipts & Refunds

Transparent checkout and itemized digital consultation slips.

  • Consultation and queue booking fees displayed before checkout include clinic-specified charges.
  • Eligible cancellations and clinic-initiated slot changes trigger refunds back to your original payment source via Razorpay.
  • Digital payment receipts and refund slips remain accessible inside Payment History.

04 Reviews & Medical Disclaimer

QueueFlow facilitates coordination, not direct medical advice.

  • Medical advice, diagnoses, and treatments are provided solely by independent clinics and licensed doctors.
  • Ratings and reviews must reflect genuine clinic visits and remain respectful and factual.
  • In life-threatening emergencies, seek immediate in-person emergency medical care rather than waiting in queue.
